Install HustlePop from the [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/hustlepop). The app embeds itself. You do not paste a script into the theme.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Add HustlePop to your store from the listing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the offer">
    Add a headline, a short line, and an optional discount. Preview the popup on desktop and on a phone before you publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Pick a trigger and publish. New emails show up in Shopify customers.
  </Step>
</Steps>
