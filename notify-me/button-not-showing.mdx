---
title: "The Notify me button is missing"
sidebarTitle: "Button not showing"
description: "Why the Notify me trigger does not appear, and how to fix it."
keywords: ["button not showing", "app embed", "widget", "trigger"]
icon: "eye-off"
---

The trigger only loads when the **app embed** is on, the **widget** is **ON** and **Published**, and the product matches your display rules.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The app embed is Off">
    **Cause:** The embed is not enabled on the published theme.

    **Fix:** On the home dashboard, choose **Enable**, turn on **Notify Me — Back in stock**, save the theme, then **Refresh status**. See [Enable the app embed](/notify-me/enable-app-embed).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The widget is Off or still a draft">
    **Cause:** The knob is **OFF**, or you saved without **Publish**.

    **Fix:** Go to **Widgets**, turn the widget **ON**, choose **Customize**, then **Publish**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The variant is in stock">
    **Cause:** Back in stock hides the trigger when the variant is available, unless you turned on continue-selling-at-zero display.

    **Fix:** Check a sold-out variant, or turn on **Show button when shoppers can still buy at zero stock** if those products stay purchasable at 0. See [Back in stock widget](/notify-me/back-in-stock-widget).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Product targeting excludes this product">
    **Cause:** Includes or excludes hide the widget on this product or collection.

    **Fix:** In **Customize** → **Trigger**, widen **Products & collections** or clear excludes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The theme layout hides the button">
    **Cause:** The trigger sits below buy buttons, or listing pages are off.

    **Fix:** Turn on **Replace Sold Out button with Notify Me** (inline trigger), or **Show on collection, search & product listing pages**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="You enabled the embed on a draft theme">
    **Cause:** Shoppers see the published theme, not the unpublished one.

    **Fix:** Enable the embed on the **published** theme, then **Refresh status**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
