Each widget has a matching **automation**: **Back in stock** and **Price drop alert**. Open **Automations**, choose **Customize**, then edit the email step.

<Frame>
  ![Automation flow with Trigger and Email steps](/images/notify-me/automation-flow.png)
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the automation">
    Go to **Automations** and choose **Customize** on Back in stock or Price drop alert.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set subject and preview text">
    Edit **Subject line** and **Preview text**, then save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the design">
    On the email step, choose **Edit** to open the designer. Insert merge tags, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  ![Alert email designer with merge tags](/images/notify-me/email-designer.png)
</Frame>

## Merge tags

| Tag | Inserts |
| --- | --- |
| `{{store_name}}` | Your shop name |
| `{{first_name}}` | Subscriber first name (or "there" if missing) |
| `{{last_name}}` | Subscriber last name |
| `{{product_title}}` | Product title |
| `{{variant_title}}` | Variant title |
| `{{product_price}}` | Product price |
| `{{product_url}}` | Link to the product |
| `{{product_image}}` | Product image |

Default subjects:

- Back in stock: `Good news — your item is back in stock`
- Price drop: `Price drop alert — your watched item just got cheaper`

The app sends these emails. Connecting Klaviyo or another ESP does **not** send the Notify Me alert. See [Integrations](/notify-me/integrations).

For bounce, open, and click hygiene after you send, see [Deliverability best practices](/notify-me/sending-domain/deliverability).
