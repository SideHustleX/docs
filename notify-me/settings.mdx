## Customer tag

**Customer tag** is added to the Shopify customer Notify Me creates or updates on signup. The default help text in the app is `notify me tag`. Use a tag you can filter in Shopify admin.

## Sender name

**Sent from Name** is the name shoppers see on the alert. The sender email stays `alerts@notify.hustlepops.com` until you verify a [custom domain](/notify-me/sending-domain/add-domain).

## Notification usage

The **Notification usage** card shows how many alerts went out **This month** and **Today**, plus how many are left.

Counts include alerts that were sent, opened, clicked, or bounced. If the meter shows that sending is paused, wait until the next UTC day or month. The bars turn caution around 80% and stop at 100%.
