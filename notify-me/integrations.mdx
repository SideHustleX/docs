Integrations connect Notify Me to your email platform. Notify Me still sends the restock and price drop **alerts**. The platform receives the **subscriber** as a contact and an **event**, so you can run your own automations.

<Warning>
  Connecting an integration does not send the Notify Me alert for you. Keep [alert emails](/notify-me/edit-alert-emails) published in Notify Me.
</Warning>

## Where to find Integrations

Open **Integrations** in the Notify Me left nav, next to Widgets, Automations, Subscribers, and Settings.

<Frame>
  ![Notify Me Integrations page with Klaviyo, Mailchimp, Omnisend, and Brevo cards](/images/notify-me/integrations.png)
</Frame>

Each card shows **Connect**. After you save a key, the card shows **Active** (or **Inactive** if you paused sync) and the button becomes **Manage**.

Need a tool that is not listed? Use **Need a custom integration? Contact us** on this page to open chat.

## What syncs

When the connection is **Active**:

1. A shopper becomes a **subscriber** — Notify Me upserts the contact and records a join event.
2. Notify Me sends a **back in stock** or **price drop** alert — the platform records that event on the contact.

You pick a list, audience, or tag when you connect. That is where new contacts land.

## Connect an integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an API key">
    Open the page for your platform and create a key there: [Klaviyo](/notify-me/integrations/klaviyo), [Mailchimp](/notify-me/integrations/mailchimp), [Omnisend](/notify-me/integrations/omnisend), or [Brevo](/notify-me/integrations/brevo).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Connect">
    In Notify Me, open **Integrations** and choose **Connect** on that card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the key and test">
    Paste the API key, choose **Test**, then pick the list, audience, or tag.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Choose **Save**. The card shows **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
