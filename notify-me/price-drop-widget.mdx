The **Price drop alert** widget lets shoppers subscribe when they want a lower price. Notify Me sends an **alert** when that variant's price goes **down**, not when you only set a compare-at price.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn the widget on">
    Go to **Widgets**, turn **Price drop alert** **ON**, and choose **Customize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the trigger and popup">
    Edit the trigger label and the [popup](/notify-me/popup) the same way as Back in stock.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Choose **Publish** so the widget loads on the storefront.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  ![Price drop alert widget editor](/images/notify-me/price-drop-editor.png)
</Frame>

<Note>
  The alert compares the new Shopify price to the price stored when the shopper subscribed. A compare-at or "on sale" badge alone does not send the email.
</Note>

You can show the trigger on [collection and listing pages](/notify-me/back-in-stock-widget#where-to-show) with **Show on collection, search & product listing pages**.

See [When alerts send](/notify-me/when-alerts-send) for the exact send rules.
