By default, alerts send from `alerts@notify.hustlepops.com` with the name **Notify Me**. Add a custom domain in **Settings** to send from your brand.

<Frame>
  ![Settings email section with sender name and custom domain](/images/notify-me/settings-email.png)
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a custom domain">
    Go to **Settings**, choose **Add a custom domain**, enter the domain only (for example `yourstore.com`), then **Register domain**. Do not enter an email address, URL, or `www`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add DNS records">
    Open **View DNS records**. Add the DKIM **TXT** record and the bounce **CNAME** at your domain provider. Use the Host field exactly as shown. Do not append your domain again if your provider adds it automatically.

    Pick your provider and follow the steps: [Cloudflare, GoDaddy, Shopify, and more](/notify-me/sending-domain/dns-providers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check verification">
    Propagation can take up to 48 hours. Choose **Check verification** until the domain shows as verified.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the sender name">
    After the domain is verified, edit **Sent from Name** and the local part of the sender email, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  ![DNS records modal with DKIM and bounce CNAME](/images/notify-me/dns-records.png)
</Frame>

<Warning>
  You cannot change the sender email until the domain is verified. Until then, alerts keep using the default Notify Me sender.
</Warning>

If **Register domain** fails with **DE_150**, see [DE_150 anti-spam check](/notify-me/sending-domain/de-150). Do not keep retrying the same domain.

<Note>
  Shopify development stores cannot register a custom domain or send alert emails.
</Note>

After the domain is verified, see [Deliverability best practices](/notify-me/sending-domain/deliverability) for bounce, open, and click hygiene.
