Open **Widgets** in the Notify Me nav. Each widget has a knob to turn it **ON** or **OFF**, and **Customize** to edit the trigger, popup, and success state.

Shoppers only see a widget when all of these are true:

- The [app embed](/notify-me/enable-app-embed) is on
- The widget knob is **ON**
- You have chosen **Publish** (not only saved a draft)

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Back in stock">
    Captures subscribers when a variant is unavailable, then sends a restock alert. See [Back in stock widget](/notify-me/back-in-stock-widget).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Price drop alert">
    Captures subscribers who want a lower price, then sends an alert when that variant's price goes down. See [Price drop widget](/notify-me/price-drop-widget).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Publish vs draft

In the widget editor, **Publish** writes the widget to your storefront. A **Draft** badge means shoppers still see the last published version, or nothing if you have never published.

<Tip>
  After you publish, check a sold-out product (or a watched product for price drop) in a private browser window.
</Tip>
