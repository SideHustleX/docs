Notify Me is the Shopify app **Back in stock alerts, Notify me**. Shoppers tap a **Notify me** trigger on your store, leave an email or phone number in the popup, and become a **subscriber**. When stock returns or a price drops, Notify Me sends the **alert**.

You need both of these for the trigger to appear:

1. The **app embed** turned on in your published theme
2. At least one **widget** enabled and **Published**
