The **Back in stock** widget shows a **Notify me** trigger when a variant is unavailable. Shoppers open the [popup](/notify-me/popup), subscribe, and wait for a restock [alert](/notify-me/when-alerts-send).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the widget">
    Go to **Widgets**, keep **Back in stock** **ON**, and choose **Customize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the trigger">
    On the **Trigger** tab, set label, colors, and where the button appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Choose **Publish**. A draft does not update the storefront.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where to show

On the **Trigger** tab, under **Where to show**:

- **Only show on out-of-stock variants** — hides the trigger when the variant is in stock. Leave this on for most stores.
- **Show on collection, search & product listing pages** — adds the trigger on product cards in grids.
- **Replace Sold Out button with Notify Me** — swaps your theme's sold-out add-to-cart control for the Notify me trigger (inline trigger only). When off, Notify me sits below the buy buttons.

## Products and collections

Use **Products & collections** to include or exclude products and collections. If you add includes, the widget only appears on those products or collection members. Excludes always win.

## Inventory rules

Most stores leave these off except the stock threshold.

<Frame>
  ![Inventory rules on the Back in stock Trigger tab](/images/notify-me/inventory-rules.png)
</Frame>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Minimum stock before sending alerts">
    Wait until at least this many units are back before emailing subscribers. Use `0` to alert on any restock.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Show button when shoppers can still buy at zero stock">
    Turn this on if some products stay purchasable at 0 stock in Shopify (**Continue selling when out of stock**) but you still want signups.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Send alerts for products that do not track quantity">
    Shopify may not update stock reliably when quantity tracking is off. Leave this off unless you know you need it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Send alerts for products that allow buying at zero stock">
    Restock emails for continue-selling products are usually unnecessary. Turn this on only if you still want alerts when inventory comes back.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
