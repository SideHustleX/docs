Wishlist adds a heart on product cards and product pages, and a **Save for later** action in the cart. Shoppers can keep items they are not ready to buy, including the variant and quantity they already chose.

Signed-in customers keep lists across devices. Guests keep a list in the browser and can move it to an account later.
