---

Install Wishlist from the [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/wishlist-save-for-later-1).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Add Wishlist to your store. The heart block and the cart action are ready for your theme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place the heart">
    Add the heart block to product cards in the theme editor. **Save for later** appears next to each cart line. Preview the store, then save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let shoppers save">
    Shoppers can start lists right away. You can turn on sale or cart reminders later if you want them.
  </Step>
</Steps>
